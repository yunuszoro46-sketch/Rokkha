The full, current design system is **[docs/ROKKHA_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](../../../docs/ROKKHA_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)**
("Earth & Emerald — Environmental Intelligence", adopted 19 November 2026). This file stays as the skill's
master entry so page overrides in `pages/` can be added later; there are none yet.

Summary for quick reference:

- Brand: forest `#173D32`, emerald `#247A5B` (primary actions), slate-green text `#344B45`, ivory page
  `#F4F0E6`, white cards, sand `#E5D8C3`, mist `#DCEBE5`, moss `#71866A`, clay `#B87955`, river `#6FA9B5`.
- Flood data scale (separate from the brand): Alert `#B3392D`, Watch `#E9B949`, Low `#CFD9CC` (never a safe
  green), No forecast `#C9C5BA` with a dashed edge, always with a legend.
- Gradients: earth (hero), forest (feature card, closing CTA), river (pipeline section). Not on ordinary cards.
- Type: Manrope headings, Inter text, IBM Plex Mono for data labels, Noto Sans Bengali for Bangla.
- Navigation: Home · Flood intelligence · Forecasts & alerts · Get help · Rescue teams · About.

History: generated red/pink proposal rejected; forest-green interim; "Calm Humanitarian" teal (18 Nov) was
replaced after review feedback that it was too plain and too rescue-led.
